### It does _nothing_?

Most mods try to change your game. This one challenges the entire Minecraft architecture (jk).

So, why would you have to use it? It runs ANYWHERE and EVERYWHERE! You can list in on your new or old modpack as a joke or just add it to your server (also, as a joke). Since it eats 0 MB RAM and has only 7 KB why not?

<img src="https://tr7zw.github.io/uikit/divider_faded/Divider_03.png" alt="Minecraft totem themed divider">
